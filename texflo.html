HOW IT WORKS

One thread. Five departments.

Every sample moves through the same line. Texflo just makes sure that motion is visible, from the moment an indent is raised to the moment it ships.

01 · Intake

Store

Fabric and trims are received against the indent number, the single ID that follows the sample for the rest of its life.

02 · Cutting

Cutting

Panels are cut to pattern. Progress updates the moment a batch clears the floor, automatically, no paperwork.

03 · Sewing

Sewing

The longest stage, and the one most likely to slip. Texflo compares elapsed time against SLA automatically.

04 · Finishing

Finishing

Pressing, checks, and final trims. The sample is one stage from done, and everyone downstream can see it.

05 · Dispatch

Dispatch

Sent to buyer. The full timeline, every stage, every hour, stays on record for the next one.
